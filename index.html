Study 1

Everyday
econometricians.

Barron, Huck & Jehiel · American Economic Journal: Microeconomics, 2024

The experiment

An investment game.
You learn from other players.

But you only see the projects
they chose to go ahead with.

The bad ones were stopped. They never appear in the data.

Barron, Huck & Jehiel (2024), AEJ: Micro 16(3), 162–198
Result

Overoptimism.

Players treated selected data as the full picture, and invested too much.

Stronger
when the others are
more sophisticated
Weaker
when information is
more correlated

Study 2

What you see
is all there is.

Estimate a hidden number. Some signals are hidden by a rule, and everyone is told the rule.

Another group sees every signal. Same information, different view.

Enke (2020), The Quarterly Journal of Economics 135(3), 1363–1398
60%

of participants were either fully rational, or fully ignored what they could not see.

For many, the missing data did not even come to mind.

More complex problem → more neglect.
